Requested by Connected agent

The connected agent provider will receive the assignment information it reads, including descriptions and grade weights, plus saved lists and priority rules. The agent may create assignments with due dates, change local completion, and manage study sessions. It cannot change the due date on an existing assignment or submit work to Canvas.

Allow this agent to:
  • Read your saved assignments, descriptions, grade weights, lists, and priority rules.
  • Create assignments and change whether your assignments are complete in this app.
  • Create, reschedule, read, and cancel saved study sessions.

This connection reads saved app data; it does not sync with Canvas. Your assignment due dates stay separate from study sessions.
